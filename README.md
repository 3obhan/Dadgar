## ⚖️ Product Overview

**Mr. J (جناب قاضی)** is an impartial two-sided dispute arbitration system engineered to evaluate competing claims without bias or diplomatic 50/50 hedging:

- **Deterministic + Rational Reasoning Pipeline:** Verifies objective mathematical, physical, chronological, and orthographic claims deterministically alongside pure logical scope analysis (`∀` vs `∃`).
- **Universal Cross-Cultural Principles:** Built around shared human invariants—natural and mathematical consistency, observable evidence, direct causality, and explicit mutual consent—free from jurisdiction-specific bias.
- **Voice & Document Support:** Supports live spoken testimony, audio uploads, evidentiary notes, and collaborative two-party defense submission links.
- **Official Stamped Decree Export:** Renders final rulings onto a formal letterhead PDF template directly in the browser.
